# Grief as the completion of love

[中文](#zh) · [English](#en)

## 中文 {#zh}

<div lang="zh-Hans" markdown="1">
### 哀伤怎样让一份爱走到终点

> “But what is grief, if not love persevering?”
>
> ——幻视（Vision）对旺达说，[《WandaVision》第 8 集〈Previously On〉](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-90affd1f-0851-48bc-9cab-c142d5c9c20c)

{% include love-cover.html language="zh" %}

爱着一个人的时候，碰上一件有趣的事，我们的第一反应常常是「这得告诉他」。一段关系结束后，这个念头有时还是会冒出来。等我们回过神，才想起那个人已经没法再听我们说了。我们明明早就知道关系已经结束，可有些时候，还是会下意识地给对方留个位置。

我想从这个小小的落差说起。一段关系结束之后，那份爱可能还在。哀伤（grief）或许就是这份爱在失去之后延续的样子。那么，当这段哀伤慢慢走到尽头，原来那份爱又发生了什么？爱情的结束总是带着哀伤吗？

### 1. 我们是在想念一个人，还是在等他？ {#zh-love-after-loss}

我们也许还记得他在某些时候的小动作，或者他遇到什么事会说哪句话，有时甚至会发现，自己处理事情的方式还带着他的影响。可无论我们猜得再准，也只有他自己才能给出一个新的回答，说一句我们根本没想到的话。记忆能留住那些细节，也能让一个人的影响延续下去。那种还有来有往的日子，一旦结束，便没法靠记忆重现。

我想弄清的是，那个人在我们眼下的生活里还占着怎样的位置。我们可以一直关心他，也可以一直珍惜两个人的过去。遇到一件从前会和他商量的事，如今也许已经能自己拿主意了。也可能即使作了决定，还是觉得少了他的回应，事情就没有真正定下来。后一种情形里，原来那份爱可能还在影响我们怎么过眼下的日子。

{% include love-conversation.html language="zh" %}

### 2. 爱情的终结有两种方式 {#zh-two-endings}

设想两个人都在失去伴侣之后平静了下来。一个人渐渐失去了当初维系这份爱的牵挂，我把这种结束叫作消解（dissolution）。另一个人依然能体会那段关系的分量，只是在一次次遇到对方的缺席之后，慢慢改变了原来的期待。我把这种结束叫作爱情的内在完成（intrinsic completion）。两个人此刻都很平静，但那份爱是怎么结束的，要看他们各自走过的路。

一段关系也可以先于爱结束。决定分开时，我们可能很清楚为什么要分手，心里却依然给对方留着一块地方。离开是可以作出的决定，爱什么时候结束，要到后来才看得出来。

{% include love-flow.html language="zh" %}

### 3. 哀伤与爱，可以在同一次转变中完成 {#zh-one-ending}

小明和小红曾一起翻译一本书。小明去世后，小红接着译。碰上一句拿不准的话，她还是会想先放着，等小明来挑挑毛病。生活里还有别的时刻会这样。她想告诉他一个新发现，或者遇上一件从前两个人会商量的事，才又一次发现，那个能和她商量的人已经不在了。

慢慢地，她能把一段译文定下来，也能给一些生活里的事拿主意。换作以前，她一定会先跟小明商量。小明参与过的那些日子对她依然珍贵，她有时还会想，要是能听听他的意见就好了。可即便没有那个回答，事情也可以就此定下来。

译完一页，只能说明她在这一页上不再等小明。我们还要看，日子里的其他地方是不是也慢慢变了。假如正是一次次面对他的缺席，让她终于放下了维系那份爱的所有期待，那么哀伤的完成与爱的完成就是同一次转变。让她哀伤的，是那个她还在等、却再也等不到的回应。她终于放下的，也正是这场等待。

### 4. 为什么我认为爱的完成必须经过哀伤 {#zh-why-grief}

小红早就知道小明不会回来。可从「我知道他不会回答」到「这件事可以就此定下来」，中间还有一段路。在我看来，只要那份爱还在这样的等待里延续，它就只有经过哀伤，才能真正走到尽头。小红早已知道等不到回答，可碰上从前会留给小明的事，她还是会下意识地等他。直到她在这些时刻一次次体会到他的缺席，才慢慢发现，有些事现在可以由她一个人定下来。

多年以后，她也许会读到一句小明改过的话，忽然很想他，也很庆幸他曾经出现在自己的生活里。她可以合上书，去做今天的事。那句话还是他们一起写出来的。

## English {#en}

> “But what is grief, if not love persevering?”
>
> — Vision, speaking to Wanda in [*WandaVision*, episode 8, “Previously On”](https://www.disneyplus.com/browse/entity-90affd1f-0851-48bc-9cab-c142d5c9c20c)

{% include love-cover.html language="en" %}

When we’re in love, we can hear something funny and immediately think, “I have to tell them.” That thought can still come to us after the relationship ends. A moment later, we remember that we can’t tell them anymore. We may have known for a long time that it’s over. Still, in moments like these, we make room for the other person without thinking.

I want to start with that small gap between what we know and what we still do. A relationship can end while the love continues. Perhaps grief is how that love carries on after loss. If the grief eventually runs its course, what happens to the love that lived on in it? Does love always end in grief?

### 1. Do we miss them, or are we still waiting for them? {#en-love-after-loss}

We might remember a small gesture, or know exactly what someone would have said in a particular situation. We might even catch ourselves doing something their way. But however well we know them, only they could give us an answer we hadn’t thought of. Memory can keep those details alive, and their influence can stay with us for years. The give-and-take of those days is harder to recover. It took both of us.

What I want to understand is the place that person still has in our lives. We can care about them and cherish what we shared. Faced with something we once would have talked over together, we may find we can make up our own minds. Or we may make a decision and still feel that it won’t quite be settled until we hear from them. When that happens, the old love may still be shaping how we live now.

{% include love-conversation.html language="en" %}

### 2. Two ways love can end {#en-two-endings}

Imagine two people who have found some peace after losing a partner. For one, the feelings that once kept the love alive gradually faded. I call this *dissolution*. For the other, the relationship still carries weight. As they kept encountering their partner’s absence, what they expected from life slowly changed. I call this love’s *intrinsic completion*. Both people are at peace now. To understand how their love ended, we have to look at how each of them got there.

A relationship can also end before the love does. We can be clear about why we’re breaking up and still find ourselves making room for the other person in our lives. We can decide to leave. It may take much longer to see when the love ends.

{% include love-flow.html language="en" %}

### 3. How grief and love can come to an end together {#en-one-ending}

Xiaoming and Xiaohong were translating a book together. After Xiaoming dies, Xiaohong keeps working on it. Whenever she gets stuck on a sentence, her first instinct is still to set it aside for Xiaoming to pick apart. It happens away from the book, too. She comes across something she wants to tell him, or faces a decision they once would have talked through. Each time, it hits her again that he isn’t there.

In time, she finds she can settle on a translation and make some of those everyday decisions herself. Once, she would have talked them over with Xiaoming first. She still treasures the time they shared. Sometimes she wishes she could hear what he thinks. She can make a decision even when the answer never comes.

Finishing a page tells us that Xiaohong has stopped waiting for Xiaoming on that page. We would have to look at the rest of her life to see how far the change has gone. If coming up against his absence again and again lets her relinquish all the expectations that kept that love alive, then grief and love reach completion in the same change. She grieves because she’s still waiting for an answer that will never come. It is that waiting she finally lets go of.

### 4. Why I think love can reach completion only through grief {#en-why-grief}

Xiaohong has long known that Xiaoming won’t come back. But there’s a distance between “I know he won’t answer” and “I can go ahead and decide.” As long as that love lives on in this kind of waiting, I think grief is the only way it can come to completion. Xiaohong knows no answer is coming. Still, whenever something comes up that she once would have saved for Xiaoming, she catches herself waiting for him. Feeling his absence in those moments, again and again, is how she slowly discovers that some things are hers to decide now.

Years later, Xiaohong might come across a sentence Xiaoming revised and suddenly miss him. She might feel grateful that he was part of her life. She can close the book and get on with her day. They still wrote that sentence together.
