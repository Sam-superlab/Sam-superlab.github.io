# About Me

*“Wir müssen wissen. Wir werden wissen.” (We must know. We will know.) — David Hilbert*

I'm Xuyi (Sam) Ren, originally from China. I study mathematics and conduct research at [Grinnell College](https://www.grinnell.edu/).

Through Grinnell's [Mentored Advanced Project (MAP)](https://www.grinnell.edu/about/leadership/offices-services/dean/maps) program, I worked with [Jennifer Kenkel](https://www.grinnell.edu/user/kenkeljennifer) on the [strong persistence property (SPP)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpaa.2014.05.011) in commutative algebra. Read more about the project on my [research page](/research/#strong-persistence-property-spp).

I also enjoy exploring mathematical patterns and sharing what I learn. My research with Christian Roettger led to the preprint [Ternary Digits of Powers of Two](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03861).

I also care about making educational resources accessible. I created [Sam's Superlab](https://ssr-web-sam.vercel.app/), a platform for sharing student notes and study resources. Read more about the platform on my [projects page](/teams/).
